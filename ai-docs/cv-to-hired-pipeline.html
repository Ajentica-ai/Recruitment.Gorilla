CV-to-hired pipeline

From the moment an Admin or Recruiter drops a CV in, to the moment a candidate is marked Hired. Both roles can upload and review drafts; only Admin and Super Admin can use the JSON + CVs import. Every upload or import now requires an open job opening, and a Recruiter can review and approve a draft only if they uploaded it themselves or are assigned to that opening. Parsing is an automatic step inside one upload request; everything after Uploaded is an explicit status change recorded by hand, except two offer outcomes a background service writes on the candidate's behalf.
