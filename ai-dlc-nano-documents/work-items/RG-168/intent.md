# Floating Upload CVs button overlaps the dashboard on phones
- Source: issue #168 https://github.com/Ajentica-ai/Recruitment.Gorilla/issues/168 (screenshots; label bug)
- Type: bug
## Request
- On a phone-width dashboard the floating Upload CVs button covers the Applications card.
## Decisions
- Remove the floating button entirely (user decision).
- Keep the hero's Candidates and Upload CVs buttons exactly as they are (shown from 768px up, hidden on phones); add nothing on phones (user confirmed). Phones reach Upload CVs via the menu.
## Out of scope
- A replacement phone shortcut.
